## 🎯 The 5 Core Cultural Features

### 1. 💌 "Soft Decline" & Traditional Digital Shagun (*Lifafa*)
- **Cultural Context:** Saying "No" to an Indian wedding invite carries strong social guilt, often causing guests to ghost or verbally commit without attending.
- **Lavish Soft Decline:** Clicking *"Cannot Attend"* opens an ornate velvet & gold modal:
  > *"We will miss you dearly! Leave your blessings for the couple."*
- **Digital Shagun Rails:** Traditional Indian wedding envelope (*lifafa*) component with auspicious denominations ending in ₹1 (**₹501, ₹1,101, ₹2,101, ₹5,001**) + simulated instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM).
- Transforms an awkward decline into a celebrated blessing and monetary gift.

### 2. 🛡️ Hard Plus-One Cap (Royal Boundary Control)
- **Cultural Context:** Extended families and plus-ones expanding unannounced, straining banquet seating and plate budgets.
- **Precision Stepper:** Hard-bounded by `maxGuestsAllowed`. Exceeding the invite limit displays an Annapurna SDG 12 notice.
- **Host Boundary Request:** Guests can click **"Request +1"** with an explanatory note (e.g., elderly grandparent needing assistance).
- **Host Command Approval:** Live queue on the Host Command Dashboard allowing 1-click seat cap elevation or polite rejection.

### 3. 🗓️ Multi-Ceremony Master Schedule & Dietary Matrix
Four distinct royal wedding ceremonies, each with individualized attendance toggles and dietary splits:
1. **Haldi & Phoolon Ki Mehendi** *(Day 1 Afternoon • Marigold Yellow & Floral Silk)*
2. **The Royal Sangeet & Musical Night** *(Day 1 Evening • Regal Velvet & Sparkle)*
3. **Auspicious Pheras & Varmala** *(Day 2 Morning • Traditional Crimson & Ivory Gold)*
4. **The Imperial Grand Reception Gala** *(Day 2 Evening • Imperial Black Tie & Banarasi Sarees)*

**Dietary Choices:** *Shakahari Pure Veg*, *Royal Non-Veg*, and *Pure Jain (Strictly no onion, garlic, or root vegetables)*.

### 4. 👨‍🍳 Live Palace Kitchen & Catering Portal (Zero-PII)
- **Zero-PII Safety Shield:** Caterers view only raw culinary aggregates, dietary percentage splits, and fire counts. No personal names, phone numbers, or monetary values are disclosed.
- **Smart 5% Safety Buffer:** Replaces blind 30% over-preparation waste with precision +5% dynamic buffer plates.
- **1-Click Printable BEO:** Generates standard official Banquet Event Order printable docket.
- **NGO Redistribution Protocol:** Surplus food dispatch scheduled with *Feeding India / Robin Hood Army* within 90 minutes.

### 5. 📱 Automated 48-Hour WhatsApp Nudge Suite
- 1-click host trigger to politely nudge unconfirmed families.
- **Tone Presets:**
  - *🌱 Annapurna Eco-Conscious Zero Waste:* Normalizes last-minute plan updates without social friction.
  - *🎉 Warm Festive Celebration:* Uplifting reminder highlighting dress codes and dance rehearsals.
